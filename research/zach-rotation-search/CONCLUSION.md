## What the testing found

1. **No version of the dip rule beat simply holding the market** after costs,
   in any period, once the data was fair. The one that seemed to (S&P 500 only)
   was survivorship bias.
2. **The one strength that held everywhere: smaller drops.** On the S&P 500 as
   it was, worst drop 23% against SPY's 55% (2006-2026), at a return about
   5 points a year lower.
3. **Expectation for Experiment B's live rule:** likely to trail SPY by several
   points a year, with smaller drops in bad years.
4. **The industry momentum rule R65-1 (round 65) held up,** and all 45 tries to improve it
   (tries 12-56) failed. The last, round 98, tied it on 2000-2026 and failed the final exam (round 105).

## Left for the future

- 2000-2005 is unused; a fair test needs price data that includes delisted
  companies.
- Experiment B continues under its approved rule unless Zach and Sam decide
  otherwise; this folder doesn't change it.
